Hurricanes Set to Raise the Banner as Puck Drops September 29

Carolina opens defense of its second Stanley Cup title at home against Florida, the first game of an ESPN tripleheader that also features Boston at the New York Rangers and Vegas at Chicago (NHL.com). Toronto hosts Montreal that same night on Sportsnet, where No. 1 overall pick Gavin McKenna, taken by the Maple Leafs out of Penn State in June, is expected to make his NHL debut (ESPN). The 2026-27 season expands to 84 games per team, an all-time league high, and runs through April 10 (NHL.com).

Around the League

The league's move to an 84-game schedule is the biggest format change in years, and it comes with new Monday games built for international viewers. On the ice, Gavin McKenna became just the sixth NCAA player ever taken No. 1 overall when Toronto selected him in June, and he enters camp as one of the most talked-about rookies in recent memory. Off the ice, the PWHL expands to 12 teams for 2026-27, with training camps opening in November across five host markets, including new additions Las Vegas and San Jose (PWHL).
